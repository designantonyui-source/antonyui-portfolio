About

I'm Anton, a Senior Product Designer with 5+ years shipping digital products across fintech, energy, and B2B SaaS. My focus is the infrastructure layer — design systems and DesignOps that let teams move fast without breaking consistency.

Most recently I was Product Designer at PGWay, a global payment gateway (150+ payment methods), where I owned product design and the design system on a large, compliance-sensitive platform. Before that, three years at TWNTY Ventures (Germany) on clients including priWatt, Mission Zero, and Tilray — and earlier, dashboard and CMS products with Natanek Iberia in Barcelona. Based in Poland — open to remote EU roles and relocation.
