Profile

I'm Anton, a Senior Product Designer with 5+ years shipping digital products across fintech, energy, and B2B SaaS. My focus is the infrastructure layer — design systems and DesignOps that let teams move fast without breaking consistency.

Most recently I was Product Designer at PGWay, a global payment gateway (150+ payment methods), where I owned product design and the design system on a large, compliance-sensitive platform. Before that, three years at TWNTY Ventures (Germany) on clients including priWatt, Mission Zero, and Tilray — and earlier, dashboard and CMS products with Natanek Iberia in Barcelona.

I care about the parts of design that don't demo well: token architecture, naming, documentation, and the handoff contract between design and engineering. That's where consistency is either won or lost.

How I work

A.

Token architecture

Three-tier tokens in Token Studio — primitives, semantics, components. One source of truth, and no hardcoded values inside a component.
B.

Naming that survives production

Conventions built to hold up after handover, not just inside the Figma file. If a name needs a comment to be understood, it's the wrong name.
C.

Documentation as a deliverable

Guidelines, usage rules and decision logs shipped alongside the components — so the system outlives the project it was built for.
D.

The handoff contract

An explicit agreement between design and engineering: what ships as a component, what stays a token, and what never gets rebuilt twice.
